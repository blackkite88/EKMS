# OEM Manual — KSB RPH-200 Centrifugal Pump

**Applies to:** P-101, P-102

## Mechanical Seal Replacement (Section 7.4)
After replacing the mechanical seal, the following steps are **MANDATORY**:
1. Reinstall the seal per torque spec.
2. **Verify shaft-to-driver alignment using a dial indicator or laser tool. Misalignment after seal work is the leading cause of premature bearing failure.**
3. Record alignment readings in the maintenance log.

> ⚠️ WARNING: Skipping the post-seal alignment verification (Step 2) will lead to accelerated bearing wear and seizure.

## Bearing Life
Rated L10 bearing life assumes alignment within 0.05 mm. Operation above rated flow (220 m³/h) further reduces bearing life.
