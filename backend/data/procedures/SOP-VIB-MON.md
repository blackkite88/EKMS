# SOP-VIB-MON — Vibration Monitoring

**Per:** OISD-STD-106

## Requirement
Rotating equipment in hydrocarbon service shall have vibration readings taken every 3 months. Readings exceeding alarm thresholds MUST be escalated to maintenance engineering within 24 hours.

**Related:** OISD-STD-106, INS-311
