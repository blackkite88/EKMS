# Factory Act, 1948 — Maintenance & Safety (excerpted)

## Requirement
Employers shall maintain plant and machinery (e.g. P-101, HX-205, V-410) in a safe condition and keep records of maintenance and inspection. Hazardous work requires documented safe systems of work.

**Applies to:** P-101, HX-205, V-410, F-101 (all equipment)
