# OISD-GDN-178 — Risk Based Inspection

## Guidance
Inspection intervals may be optimised using risk-based methodology considering criticality and damage mechanisms for equipment such as V-410, HX-205, P-101.

**Applies to:** V-410, HX-205, P-101 (all equipment)
