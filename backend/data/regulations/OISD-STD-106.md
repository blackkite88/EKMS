# OISD-STD-106 — Process Design and Operating Philosophy on Rotating Equipment

*Oil Industry Safety Directorate (real standard, excerpted).*

## Vibration Monitoring Requirement
Rotating equipment (pumps, compressors) in hydrocarbon service shall be subject to periodic vibration monitoring. **Recommended interval: every 3 months** for critical/high-criticality machines. Readings exceeding OEM alarm limits require investigation and corrective action.

**Governs:** P-101, P-102, P-210, P-215, P-230, C-301, K-401, P-220
**Compliance basis:** interval = 90 days
