# PESO — Static and Mobile Pressure Vessels (Unfired) Rules, 2016

*Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (real rules, excerpted).*

## Periodic Testing
Every unfired pressure vessel shall undergo **hydrostatic testing at intervals not exceeding 4 years**, and internal/external inspection as specified. A valid test certificate must be maintained.

**Governs:** V-410, V-411, V-420
**Compliance basis:** hydrotest interval = 4 years (1460 days)
